📧 HUEGLAM – How to Set Up Store Emails (Resend Guide)
What this does:
Whenever a customer buys something on HUEGLAM:

The customer gets an instant receipt in their email with photos of the products they bought.
You (the Admin) get an instant notification email with the customer's name, phone, delivery address, and order total.
Step 1: Create a Resend Account
Open your browser and go to resend.com/signup.
Sign up using your business email (for example: support@hueglam.com or your Google account).
Confirm your email address.
Step 2: Add Your Website Domain (hueglam.com)
Why? Email providers like Gmail will mark emails as Spam unless you prove you own hueglam.com.

Inside Resend, click on "Domains" on the left menu.
Click the black button that says "Add Domain".
Type: hueglam.com
Click "Add".
You will see a table with 3 DNS Records (they look like codes with names like DKIM, SPF, MX). Keep this tab open.
Step 3: Connect It to Your Domain (e.g. GoDaddy / Namecheap)
(If you are not sure how to do this, your domain provider's support chat can do it for you in 2 minutes!)

Log in to where you bought hueglam.com (GoDaddy, Hostinger, Namecheap, Cloudflare, etc.).
Go to My Domains → DNS Settings (or Manage DNS).
Copy the 3 lines from Resend and paste them into your DNS records:
Line 1 (TXT)
Line 2 (MX)
Line 3 (TXT)
Go back to Resend and click "Verify Domain".
The status will turn Green ("Verified").
Step 4: Get Your Secret API Key
In Resend, click on "API Keys" on the left menu.
Click "Create API Key".
For Name, type: HUEGLAM Production
Click "Add".
A secret key will appear that starts with re_...
(Example: re_123456789abcdefg...)
Copy this key and send it to your developer (or save it securely).
Step 5: What Gets Added to the Website Settings:
Your developer will add these 3 lines to your store:

env
RESEND_API_KEY="re_your_copied_key_here"
ORDER_EMAIL_FROM="HUEGLAM <support@hueglam.com>"
ORDER_EMAIL_BCC="support@hueglam.com"
✅ That's it!
From that moment on:

All emails will officially be sent from HUEGLAM <support@hueglam.com>.
They will land straight in the customer’s Primary Inbox (not Spam/Junk).
Every order will alert your admin email automatically.
